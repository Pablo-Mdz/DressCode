# Code of Conduct

DressCode is a small, friendly project. The short version: be respectful, be constructive, and assume good faith.

In practice:

- Disagree about code and ideas, not about people. Critique the pull request, not the person who wrote it.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, discussions, or anywhere else related to this project.
- Keep discussions on-topic and welcoming to people with less context than you — everyone was new to this codebase once.

If someone's behavior in this project's spaces makes you uncomfortable, email **pablocigoy@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled privately. Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content, and may block people, to keep this a civil place to contribute.

This is intentionally short rather than a long formal policy — if it needs to grow, it will.
